How to review?
It's simple. Use the attached QR code to access and review the policy.

A QR code hides its destination completely — unlike a text link, you can't hover over it or preview where it leads before your phone's camera opens it. QR codes are increasingly used specifically because they also slip past email filters that scan for suspicious links in plain text.

Scanning this takes you straight to a fake sign-in page built to steal your credentials, with no chance to check the URL first the way you could with a normal link. If a QR code is unexpected, don't scan it — go to the source (like your real HR portal) directly instead.
